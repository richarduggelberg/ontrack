DONE: proximity penalty extended to Europe / outside_europe

scripts/fetch_world_coords.py geocodes all Europe/outside_europe
destinations (country-code-disambiguated via Nominatim), cached in
data/world_coords_cache.json (343/355 resolved; 12 landmark-like
entries - e.g. Louvren i Paris, Marianergraven - have no real "lat/lon
city" and are left unresolved/no-penalty).

build_predictions.py's proximity logic (local-density-scaled radius,
lookback+decay, weight exponent) is now region-generic (PROXIMITY_REGIONS
= sweden/europe/outside_europe) with per-region radius min/max clamps
(europe: 150-1200km, outside_europe: 300-3000km) since the scale of
"too close" is very different across regions.

Historical-pattern validation (consecutive-episode distance vs. random
pairs, same check used for Sweden) was weaker here than for Sweden:
- europe: a real but modest avoidance signal (13.7% of consecutive
  picks fall under their own density-scaled radius vs. 15.5% for random
  pairs) - same direction as Sweden, much smaller effect size.
- outside_europe: no avoidance pattern found - consecutive picks are
  if anything slightly *more* likely to be close than random pairs
  (likely because episodes are sometimes thematically grouped by
  continent/region rather than avoiding repeats). Backtesting
  (scripts/backtest_proximity.py --region outside_europe) across 3
  disjoint windows showed no consistent improvement either.

Decision (explicit user call despite the weak/absent evidence): enabled
for both regions anyway, using the same defaults as Sweden (lookback=1,
weight=1.0, decay=0.5) rather than overfitting to noisy per-region
backtest numbers.
